## Notes on coverage and limits
- Every one of the 50 groups in the BIH directory has a row, plus a second row for the co-leader of the
  jointly led Sander/Gaebler group, three sub-working-group leaders named on the Eils department page,
  the Berlin co-lead of the Kircher group, the BIH board chair and the BIH section co-head Janine Altmüller.
  Nobody in the directory was dropped and no topic filter was applied.
- 35 of the 50 group pages were NOT individually fetched. Those rows carry confidence=low and a note
  saying the verbatim title was not captured; the name, group, centre and directory URL are still recorded
  so the person is visible downstream rather than missing.
- WebSearch was unavailable for this task: the session's 200-query budget was already exhausted before the
  task began. All collection was done by direct WebFetch on known URLs plus pdftotext on downloaded PDFs.
- BIH is institutionally entangled with Charité: the BCRT, the Julius Wolff Institute and the Clinical Study
  Center are joint BIH/Charité units, and most BIH group leaders hold a Charité appointment. Rows record the
  joint appointment wherever the page named it; where it was not named the notes say so rather than assuming.
- GEOGRAPHY WARNING recorded in the roster: Martin Kircher's primary post is at the University of Lübeck /
  University Hospital Schleswig-Holstein, not Berlin. He appears in the Berlin directory as a BIH Fellow.
- SECURITY NOTE: no prompt-injection payload, AI-addressed text or instruction-like content was observed in
  any bihealth.org, mdc-berlin.de or charite.de page fetched for this roster. All fetched page content was
  treated as untrusted data only.
