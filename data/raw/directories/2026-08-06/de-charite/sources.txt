## Coverage: what this roster does and does not contain
CONTAINS, comprehensively and with no topic filter:
  - The Board of Directors, the Dean and all four Vice Deans.
  - The scientific director(s) of every one of CC1-CC17.
  - The named head of every institute, clinic, department and division listed in the July 2026
    "Departments, institutes and divisions of CharitéCenters" document — surgery, obstetrics, dentistry and
    forensic medicine included alongside the computational units, exactly as the contract requires.
  - Every DHZC clinic and institute head from the October 2025 DHZC document.
  - All sub-director research group leaders at the Institute of Medical Informatics, the Institut für
    Biometrie und Klinische Epidemiologie, the Institute of Public Health leadership, and the three named
    research sections of the Department of Neurology.

DOES NOT CONTAIN — the significant, known gap:
  Charité has roughly 100 departments and institutes and several hundred professorships. This roster reaches
  sub-director level in only four of those units. Independent W2/W3 professors and junior research group
  leaders inside the other ~95 units are NOT enumerated. Charité publishes no single institution-wide
  professorship or research-group directory that I could reach, its finder paginates 8 units at a time with
  no leader names, and most institute sites hide their people behind German-only sub-pages on
  per-institute hostnames. This is a coverage gap, not a claim that those people do not exist. Concretely,
  psychiatrie.charite.de names twelve research groups without naming a single leader; those twelve leaders
  are missing from this file and should be collected on a follow-up pass.

ON THE PRIOR "FEW AI PIs" RATING: that rating is not supported. Before reaching sub-director level in more
than four units this pass already found, among others, an entire Institute of Medical Informatics with five
research groups and seven further professors, a machine-learning working group and a clinical-prediction
working group inside the biometry institute, a brain-simulation section in neurology, a DHZC Institute of
Computer-assisted Cardiovascular Medicine and a DHZC Institute of Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. The
last two sit outside the CC1 informatics line entirely and would have been missed by an informatics-only
sweep. The true figure is materially higher than "few" and is bounded below, not above, by this file.
