## STRUCTURE - what the institute calls its own units
https://hertie.ai/the-institute/about-us says "the three research Departments are structured around
supporting Central Structures". The site's own navigation names three research pillars, and the third
is called Independent Research Groups rather than a department:
  1. Data Science - Director Philipp Berens. Profiles say "Department of Data Science".
     Three research groups: Machine Learning for Safe Medical Diagnostics, Neuronal Modeling,
     Representation Learning and Data Visualization.
  2. Machine Learning - Director Kerstin Ritter. Profiles say "Department of Machine Learning".
     Three research groups: Multimodal Modelling, Precision Brain Science, Translational Barriers.
  3. Independent Research Groups. Four entries: Computational Neuropsychiatry, Neural Data
     Representation, Quantitative Biomedical Image Analysis, and the Hertie AI Fellow Program.
Plus Central Structures (Central Office, Scientific Computing, Board of Trustees) - administrative.

## JOINT APPOINTMENTS
The institute IS part of the University of Tuebingen, so a university link is structural rather than
incidental: every profile career table names the employer as "Hertie Institute for AI in Brain Health,
University of Tuebingen" and staff e-mail is @uni-tuebingen.de (a few use @med.uni-tuebingen.de or
@medizin.uni-tuebingen.de, i.e. the medical faculty / university hospital). Overlap with the already
collected de-university-of-t-bingen roster is therefore expected for all ten rows, and is recorded
per row. Breakdown of the ten:
  - 2 full professors of the University of Tuebingen: Berens (Data Science), Ritter (Machine Learning
    for Clinical Neuroscience). Both are also PI/Speaker in the Excellence Cluster "Machine Learning -
    New Perspectives for Science" and in the Tuebingen AI Center, both of which are separate collected
    institutions - check de-tue-ai-center for duplicates.
  - 6 group leaders employed through the university without a named chair: Sokoloski, Schulz, Damrich,
    Kofler, Nghiem, and (see caveat) Kobak.
  - 1 whose primary chair is elsewhere: Koppe, Full Professor for Scientific Computing at Heidelberg
    University and Research Group Computational Psychiatry at the Central Institute of Mental Health,
    Medical Faculty Mannheim. She is a Hertie AI Fellow, not a Tuebingen employee; the Fellow Program
    page describes Fellows as being from "leading institutions worldwide ... whether based nearby or
    abroad". She should probably count at Heidelberg.
  - 1 administrative: Buerger, Managing Director, previously Medical Faculty and Central
    Administration of the University of Tuebingen.
  - Historic, not current: Ritter and Schulz both came from Charite - Universitaetsmedizin Berlin
    (Ritter to 2024, Schulz to 2024) and several of their group members still carry @charite.de
    addresses, which is a stale-affiliation trap rather than a live joint appointment.
No group leader on this roster holds a University Hospital Tuebingen appointment that the site names,
despite the institute's clinical focus; the hospital link appears instead as collaboration language
("We collaborate closely with the university hospital", "Department of Ophthalmology").
