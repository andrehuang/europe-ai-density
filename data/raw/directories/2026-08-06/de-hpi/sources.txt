SECURITY NOTE: no prompt-injection payload was found on any hpi.de or uni-potsdam.de page
fetched for this roster. Every fetched HTML file was scanned for hidden-text CSS
(display:none / visibility:hidden), long base64 blobs, injected console output and text
addressing an AI agent or instructing it to ignore instructions. The only hits anywhere in
this pass were benign anchor divs on a PIK page (see de-pik/sources.txt). All page content
was treated as untrusted data.

NOTE ON THE URL GIVEN IN THE TASK: https://hpi.de/en/research/chairs.html redirects to
https://hpi.de/en/research/chairs/ which returns HTTP 404. The live equivalent is the German
page https://hpi.de/forschung/fachgebiete/ , which is also more complete: it plus the
Professorium page https://hpi.de/forschung/professorium/ together give 33 current chair
holders (the Fachgebiete list omits Kay Giesecke; the Professorium includes him). hpi.de
returns 403 to the WebFetch tool but serves normally to curl with a browser User-Agent.

ENTITY / SCOPE NOTE: the University of Potsdam's Digital Engineering Fakultät chair list
(https://www.uni-potsdam.de/de/digital-engineering/forschung/fachgebiete) contains one
professor who is NOT an HPI Fachgebietsleiter:in — Prof. Dr. Dagmar Kainmueller, whose link
points to the Max Delbrück Center (https://www.mdc-berlin.de/de/kainmueller). She is
therefore recorded in the University of Potsdam roster, not here. The same page also lists
the emeriti Hasso Plattner and Christoph Meinel; hpi.de lists Plattner, Meinel, Werner Zorn
and Siegfried Wendt under "Emeriti und entpflichtete Professoren" and they are excluded per
the contract.
