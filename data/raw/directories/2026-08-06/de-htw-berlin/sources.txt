## Completeness and method
- HTW does not publish a plain "all professors" page; its central Personensuche is
  restricted to the HTW network. The Online-Forschungskatalog > Professor_innenprofile
  listing (A-Z above) is HTW's own university-wide professor register and yielded 309.
- All ~60 degree-programme staff pages were then swept independently. Of the 375
  people they add beyond the catalogue, only 2 carried a professor title and neither
  holds a current Hochschullehrer*in function; the rest are Lehrbeauftragte,
  wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter*innen, Laboringenieur*innen and administrative staff.
  The catalogue is therefore treated as complete for current professors.
- Roster = every person whose HTW person page states the function "Hochschullehrer*in"
  (302), plus the Präsidentin (a professor holding the university-level office), plus
  one low-confidence row (see below). Total 304.
