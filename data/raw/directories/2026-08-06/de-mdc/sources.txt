## Site methodology (requires_person_site)
The two MDC sites in this roster are roughly 13 km apart and are recorded per person:
  - MDC-BIMSB, Hannoversche Str. 28, 10115 Berlin — Berlin-Mitte
  - MDC Campus Berlin-Buch, Robert-Rössle-Str. 10 / Lindenberger Weg 80, 13125 Berlin — Berlin-Buch
Site evidence used, in descending order of strength, and stated per row in the notes column:
  1. A postal address on the person's own lab page (16 people, confidence=high).
  2. Membership of the ECRC group-leader list, whose single address is Lindenberger Weg 80, Buch (18 people, confidence=high).
  3. Membership of the MDC-BIMSB faculty list, whose address is Hannoversche Str. 28, Mitte (confidence=medium — a listing, not an address).
  4. Absence from both lists, defaulted to the Buch main campus and labelled "inferred, unverified" with confidence=low.
Phone area codes were not used: all MDC numbers share the +49 30 940 6 prefix across both sites, so they carry no site information.
IMPORTANT FINDING: the BIMSB "associated group" label does NOT predict the site. Selbach, Jana Wolf and Piazza are
labelled BIMSB associated groups but their verified addresses are at Buch, while Kainmüller, also labelled associated,
is verified at Hannoversche Str. 28 in Mitte. Only individually verified addresses should be treated as strong evidence.
Two people are marked site=UNCLEAR rather than guessed: Michael Sigal (conflicting evidence) and Philipp Mertins (no evidence).

## Notes on coverage and limits
- Every entry in the MDC labs/platforms directory has a row. The two jointly led labs (Janz/Mathas and Müller/Dechend)
  produced two rows each. Four labs whose leader name was not printed in the listing (Huppa, Sattentau, Willnow,
  Schwayer) were resolved by fetching the lab page rather than being dropped. No topic filter was applied: structural
  biologists, immunologists and clinicians are all recorded alongside the computational groups.
- People holding both a lab and a technology platform (Ralf Kühn, Tobias Pischon) and Robert Zinzen, who heads two
  platforms, are written as one row each with both roles in the title, so the project does not double count them.
- WebSearch was unavailable for this task: the session's 200-query budget was already exhausted before the task began.
  All collection was done by direct WebFetch on known URLs plus pdftotext on downloaded PDFs.
- SECURITY NOTE: no prompt-injection payload, AI-addressed text or instruction-like content was observed in any
  mdc-berlin.de page fetched for this roster. All fetched page content was treated as untrusted data only.
