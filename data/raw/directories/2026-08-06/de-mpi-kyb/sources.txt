## SECURITY
No prompt-injection payload was encountered on any page listed above. Nothing on these pages
addressed an AI agent, asked for instructions to be disregarded, or tried to redirect the task.

One item was inspected and cleared rather than assumed benign: every unit and person page carries
long base64-looking query strings on its <img>/<source> URLs (pattern ?t=ZXlKM2FXUjBhQ0k2...). These
are double-base64 image-resize tokens; decoding one yields {"width":140,"height":180,"fit":"crop",
"file_extension":"jpg","obj_id":583090}. This is normal MPG CMS markup, not hidden text. Noted here
because the same shape (base64 filler) is part of the known Fraunhofer IAO injection pattern, and a
later pass should not mistake this for one.

## DEPARTMENT vs. INDEPENDENT GROUP - what the institute itself says
The institute publishes three tiers under "Research", and the roster's notes column records which
tier each person sits in, with the URL that states it. The project rules on which tiers qualify.

1. https://www.kyb.tuebingen.mpg.de/departments - English label "Established Research Groups".
   The German site (https://www.kyb.tuebingen.mpg.de/de) titles the same menu block
   "Staendige Forschungsgruppen (Abteilungen)", i.e. it names them departments outright, and the
   English URL is literally /departments. The page text: "The Established Research Groups at the
   Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics are headed by Directors and Max Planck Fellows."
   Individual person pages print the unit as "Department Body-Brain-Cybernetics", "Department
   Computational Neuroscience", "Department High-field Magnetic Resonance", "Department Sensory and
   Sensorimotor Systems". Four units, four heads: de Araujo, Dayan, Scheffler, Zhaoping Li.
   Note that two of the four are headed by a Max Planck Fellow rather than a Director.

2. https://www.kyb.tuebingen.mpg.de/researchgroups - "Research Groups", i.e. Max Planck Research
   Groups. Page text: "Excellent young researchers ... have the opportunity to lead a Max Planck
   Research Group. This enables them to develop an independent profile and take on leadership
   responsibility. Within the Institute, they are free to work in their own research area."
   Seven groups, eight leaders (Systems Neuroscience & Neuroengineering is co-led by Li and Robson):
   Brodt, Lorenz, Breska, Ohlendorf, Spitschan, J. M. Li + Robson, Fries.
   The German group pages print the title as "Leiter/Leiterin Max-Planck-Forschungsgruppe".

3. https://www.kyb.tuebingen.mpg.de/906574/minerva-fast-track-groups - "Minerva Fast Track Groups",
   a separate tier again: four-year funding for outstanding female scientists "with the goal of
   subsequently applying to lead a Max Planck research group". One group, one leader: Zeraati.
   Her person page files her under Department Computational Neuroscience, so this group sits inside
   a department.

4. Not a published tier, but present in the staff directory: four people titled plain "Group Leader"
   whose recorded unit is a department rather than a named research group - Buckenmaier, Lohmann and
   Pohmann in High-field Magnetic Resonance, Eschenko in Computational Neuroscience. The roster marks
   these as subteams inside a department.

## ENTITY AMBIGUITY - MPI for Intelligent Systems Tuebingen (de-mpi-is-tue)
No page fetched for this roster described the MPI for Intelligent Systems. The only point of contact
is the shared-campus link "Max Planck Campus Tuebingen" in the site footer, which is infrastructure,
not organisational overlap. Every unit, person and structure page fetched is unambiguously headed
"Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics". Cross-listing with de-mpi-is-tue is therefore not
expected from these pages; the historical route by which people appear on both is the closed
Empirical Inference department (see below), which the institute lists as a FORMER department.

## OTHER GAPS AND CAVEATS
- Second affiliations are thinly documented on this site. Only Scheffler (University of Tuebingen),
  Spitschan (TUM, via a link the institute itself publishes), Lohmann (University Clinic Tuebingen,
  via her CV text) and Gangarossa (Universite Paris Cite) are stated. Dayan and Zhaoping Li almost
  certainly hold university appointments - the /departments page says the Max Planck Fellow programme
  exists to link "outstanding university teachers" with the MPG, which makes a chair implicit for
  Zhaoping Li - but the institute never names them, and they were not invented here.
- Pascal Fries is listed BOTH as leader of Research Group Neurodynamics on /researchgroups AND under
  Institutional Guests on his own person page. His employment status at this institute is genuinely
  ambiguous on the site; the roster records both placements.
- WebSearch was unavailable for this pass: the session's 200-query budget was already exhausted when
  this task started, and no search call succeeded. All collection above is direct fetching, which was
  sufficient here because the institute publishes a complete single-page staff directory.
