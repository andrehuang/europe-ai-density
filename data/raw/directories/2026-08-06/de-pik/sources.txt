SECURITY NOTE: no prompt-injection payload was found on any pik-potsdam.de page fetched.
Every fetched HTML file was scanned for hidden-text CSS, long base64 blobs, injected console
output, and text addressing an AI agent or telling it to ignore instructions. Two matches for
"visibility: hidden" occurred on
https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute/departments/complexity-science/research/time-series-analysis
and two for "display: none" on https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute/labs/pbscience ; both
were inspected and are ordinary zero-height anchor <div>s used for in-page jump links
(id="highlights", id="tocsy"), containing no text. All page content was treated as untrusted
data.

STRUCTURE NOTE: the task's directory URL points at one department only. PIK's own structure
page (https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute/departments) states research is organised in
FIVE research departments — RD1 Earth System Analysis, RD2 Climate Resilience, RD3
Transformation Pathways, RD4 Complexity Science, RD5 Climate Economics and Policy - MCC
Berlin — plus, from https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute , separate Labs, FutureLabs &
Science Units and a Policy Unit. All of these were worked through. The Labs and FutureLabs
are NOT reachable from the department pages' working-group lists, so a department-only sweep
would have missed the Planetary Boundaries Science Lab, the Land Use Transition Lab, the
Energy Transition Lab, the Policy Evaluation Lab, the Societal Transition and Well-being Lab,
and the FutureLabs ERSU, Security/Migration and Social Metabolism.

AFFILIATION NOTE: PIK's own page https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute/scientific-networks/university-cooperations
carries two tables — "Gemeinsame Berufungen / Joint appointments" and "Weitere Professuren und
Dozenten / Further professorships and lecturers". It is by far the most complete source for
the second affiliations and was used for every joint appointment recorded.

SITE NOTE: PIK has exactly two locations, and they are in different cities. Potsdam:
Telegrafenberg A 31, 14473 Potsdam. Berlin: EUREF-Campus 19, Torgauer Str. 12-15, 10829
Berlin — described by PIK as "The PIK location Berlin (MCC)" and corresponding to RD5
"Climate Economics and Policy - MCC Berlin". RD5 members are therefore recorded as Berlin,
not Potsdam. The pages fetched give no per-person desk location, so this is a department-level
inference and is stated as such in each row's notes.

ENTITY NOTE: PIK is a member of the Leibniz Association (stated on
https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/institute/about), not Helmholtz. RD5 is run jointly with the
Mercator Research Institute on Global Commons and Climate Change (MCC), a legally separate
Berlin institute; RD5 people are recorded here because PIK's own structure page counts RD5 as
one of its five research departments, with the MCC relationship named in every such row.
