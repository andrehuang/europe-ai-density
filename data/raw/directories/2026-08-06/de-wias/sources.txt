BLOCKER - READ THIS FIRST
=========================
www.wias-berlin.de was UNREACHABLE for the entire session. Evidence:
  - WebFetch of https://www.wias-berlin.de/research/rgs/ timed out (60 s) on eight separate attempts,
    across the paths /, /research/rgs/, /research/rgs/index.jsp, ?lang=0 and ?lang=1, and with and
    without the "www." prefix.
  - curl from this machine: DNS resolves (www.wias-berlin.de -> 62.141.177.111, no AAAA record);
    port 80 answers with a 301 to HTTPS; port 443 then accepts nothing and times out at 45 s and 90 s.
  - Three independent third-party reader proxies also failed to reach the origin, which rules out a
    local network block: r.jina.ai -> HTTP 422, api.allorigins.win -> HTTP 522, api.codetabs.com ->
    HTTP 521, translate.goog -> HTTP 408. 521/522 are "origin unreachable" statuses returned by the
    proxy itself.
  - A different WIAS host, archive.wias-berlin.de (62.141.177.22), does respond, so the domain is
    live; it is specifically the www host's HTTPS service that is down.
The contract allows a reader proxy as a workaround for unreachable sites. Since no live proxy could
reach the origin, this roster was built from INTERNET ARCHIVE captures of WIAS's own pages, all of
them from 2026 and most from April-June 2026, i.e. two to four months before the snapshot label.
Each roster row's notes column carries the capture date of the page it came from. If the site comes
back, this roster should be re-run against the live pages: the two "acting head" arrangements
(RG 1 and RG 6) and the April 2026 renaming of RG 7 are exactly the kind of thing that moves.

  The institute publishes: 8 numbered Research Groups (RG 1 - RG 8); a "Flexible Research Platform"
  containing two "Weierstrass group" units, two Leibniz-Group units and one "Focus platform"; a
  Director's Office; and supporting departments (Administration, IMU Office, IT, Research Data
  Management, Knowledge and Technology Transfer, Communication Design).
- Supporting-department heads (Dr. Eleanor Eife-Horn, Administration; Henrik Pletat, IT; Dr. Torsten
  Köhler, Knowledge and Technology Transfer; Thomas Bender, Communication Design; Dr. Falk Fabich,
  Acting Managing Director of the Joint Administration of Forschungsverbund Berlin) are NOT in the
  roster: they are administrative/technical staff, which the contract excludes. Dietmar Hömberg heads
  the IMU Office and IS in the roster, because he separately heads RG 4.
- Mandate holders named on the organigram (Ombudsperson, Data Protection Coordinator, Equal
  Opportunities Representative, Works Council Chairman, Representative for Disabled Employees) are not
  positions in the contract's sense and are not given rows of their own; Benedikt Jahnel appears
  because he heads a research group, not because he is Ombudsperson.
- OPEN QUESTION: Volker John's page states he has been "2. stellvertretender Direktor des WIAS" since
  10/2023. That implies a FIRST deputy director whom none of the pages fetched names. The organigram
  shows only "Director / Prof. Dr. Michael Hintermüller". This person is missing from the roster and
  should be looked for when the site is reachable again.
- OPEN QUESTION: the "Research Fellows", "Guests", "Honorary Members" and "Associated Scientists"
  listings are generated dynamically and the Internet Archive holds only their navigation shells, so
  they could not be read. Honorary members are excluded by the contract anyway, but "Associated
  Scientists" may contain independent researchers and remains unchecked.
- The RG 6 focus platform "Quantitative Analysis of Stochastic and Rough Systems" and the groups marked
  with "until" dates on the organigram are recorded as low-confidence rows rather than dropped, because
  the organigram capture is from February 2026 and the flexible platform page from May 2026 disagree
  about which of them are still running.
