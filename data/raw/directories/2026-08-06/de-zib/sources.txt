STATUS OF THE INSTITUTION (recorded from ZIB's own pages, per the task instruction not to rely on any
association membership list):
- https://www.zib.de/impressum states verbatim: "ZIB was founded as an institution incorporated under
  public law." ("Anstalt des oeffentlichen Rechts" in the German original.)
- Neither /institute, /institute/organization nor /impressum names ZIB as a member of the Leibniz
  Association, the Helmholtz Association, Max Planck or Fraunhofer. No such membership statement was
  found anywhere on the ZIB pages fetched. The earlier presumption that ZIB is a Leibniz member is not
  supported by ZIB's own site.
- /institute/organization states: "The Statutes, adopted by the Administrative Council at its meeting on
  30 June 2005, define the functions and procedures of ZIB's bodies", and names an Administrative Council
  and a Scientific Advisory Board as its governing bodies.
- The /institute page displays logos and links for MATH+, Research Campus MODAL and BIFOLD. These are
  collaborative research projects/consortia that ZIB participates in, not a parent organisation.

COVERAGE NOTES:
- ZIB's own structure page (/institute) presents three research divisions, each containing two
  departments, plus three "Research Service Units". All three divisions, all six research departments,
  all their research groups and all three service units were visited. No sub-unit list was assumed in
  advance; the divisions and departments were read off ZIB's own organisational chart.
- The department "Scientific Computing" (/research/pdc/sc) states "The department page is currently under
  construction" and lists no research groups; only its head is recorded.
- ZIB member and department pages do NOT state university appointments. Several ZIB staff carry
  professorial titles that almost certainly correspond to chairs at FU Berlin, HU Berlin, TU Berlin or
  elsewhere, but no ZIB page fetched names the university. The only joint appointment that could be
  evidenced is Sebastian Pokutta's TU Berlin professorship, and only from his own homepage, which ZIB
  links as his personal page. All other other_affiliations fields are deliberately left blank rather than
  guessed, and the affected rows carry a note flagging the de-duplication risk against the Berlin
  university rosters.
- The A-Z member index at /members was swept for every entry carrying "Prof." or "PD" so that professors
  who are not unit heads would not be lost. That sweep returned 21 people; "Özdemir, Ilyas, Prof. Dr."
  appears on the Applied Optimization department page but not in that A-Z sweep (probably an umlaut
  sorting artefact), and he is recorded from the department page instead.
