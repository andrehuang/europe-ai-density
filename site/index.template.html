    Read this before quoting a number

    Only Tübingen, Saarbrücken, Stuttgart and Kaiserslautern have been through the full protocol. Everything else is a CSRankings-only preview that inherits every gap in that source: France and Spain are badly under-counted, and the Max Planck AI institute in Tübingen is invisible to it entirely.

    Why the radius matters so much

    Tübingen reads 69.7 PIs per 100k at a 3 km radius and 1.8 at 40 km — the same 43 people, a factor of thirty-nine apart. No single radius is correct, which is why you draw the boundary rather than being handed one. 15 km is a default, not a claim.

    What the tiers mean

    T1 is a university faculty roster, T2 a research-institute PI, T3 someone recovered only by reconciling the rosters against the publication record. T3 is the contested layer, so it is a switch rather than a footnote: Tübingen loses Yong Cao and Harrisen Scells without it, and both hold real chairs there.

    The map has no tiles

    Every bright cell is inhabited ground from the population raster, which is also the denominator. Cities appear because people live in them, not because a basemap drew them.
